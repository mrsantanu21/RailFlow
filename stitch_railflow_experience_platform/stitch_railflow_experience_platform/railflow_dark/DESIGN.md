## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-premium, precision-engineered transit experience for Indian railway operations, ticketing, and live telemetry in an immersive dark theme. It combines the rigorous operational certainty of national railway infrastructure with the razor-sharp visual fidelity of mission control interfaces and high-end aviation cockpits.

### Brand Personality & Demeanor
- **Sovereign Precision & Telemetry Command:** Built on deep astronomical navy and slate containers, projecting unwavering technical reliability during rapid quota drops, Tatkal rushes, and high-frequency booking throughput.
- **Electric Legibility:** High-contrast cyan accents illuminate active telemetry, route progress, and critical interactions against a low-fatigue night palette.
- **Unambiguous Semantic Action:** Crisp emerald indicators provide instant clarity for confirmed bookings and open berths, while warm saffron-amber conveys urgency for RAC, Waitlist, and Tatkal windows without visual panic.
- **Subtle Glass & Atmospheric Depth:** Translucent deep navy layers with frosted borders soften dense logistical data into structured, scannable hierarchies.

### Design Movement & Visual Style
A tailored synthesis of **Modern High-Contrast Dark Utility** and **Tactical Glassmorphism**. Dark surfaces are stratified through precise tone containers rather than heavy pitch-black voids. Translucent deep navy glass plates, sharp neon telemetry accents, and structural slate borders construct an elite, command-center ambiance.

## Colors

The color palette prioritizes high-contrast legibility in low-light environments, strict semantic communication, and operational hierarchy. Pure black is avoided in favor of layered deep navy tones to prevent eye strain and preserve depth perception across complex route timetables.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#38BDF8` - Electric Cyan):** The primary interactive and telemetry accent. Used for focused inputs, active tab indicators, live train movement pulses, primary CTAs, and key route vectors.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Warm Amber / Saffron):** Dedicated to time-sensitive inventory and alert states: Tatkal booking windows, RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation), dynamic fare shifts, and approaching departure timers.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Vivid Emerald):** Signals positive states: confirmed berths (CNF), on-time train statuses, available quota counters, and successful transactions.
- **Neutral / Canvas (`#0B1020` - Deep Void Navy):** The absolute background canvas providing atmospheric depth and high contrast for light-emitting typography.

### Surface Tiers & Container Model
- **Base Canvas (`#0B1020`):** System floor and backdrop for full-bleed layouts.
- **Surface Container (`#141B2D`):** Resting state for cards, ticket rows, and timetable panels.
- **Surface Bright (`#1E293B`):** Hovered cards, raised action drawers, elevated dropdowns, and segmented tab active states.
- **Deep Navy Glass (`rgba(20, 27, 45, 0.75)`): Sticky navigation headers, docked booking trays, and modal sheets with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

### Border & Division Grammar
Borders are structural rather than decorative. Default dividers and card perimeters use `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`. Active, focused, or high-priority elements use an illuminated cyan rim (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy separates prominent identity markers and structural stations from dense logistical readings, using optical weights suited for luminous text on dark backdrops.

### Font Pairings & Roles
- **Headlines & Display (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Modern, geometric, and crisp. Applied to train names (e.g., *Vande Bharat Express*), primary station junction codes (`NDLS`, `BCT`), hero fare summaries, and section headers. Its distinct apertures maintain clarity under glowing cyan or white illumination.
- **Body & Tabular Numerical Data (`Inter`):** Engineered for dense transit telematics. Used for departure/arrival schedules, PNR verification strings, platform assignments, coach allocations, and distance computations. Tabular figures (`tnum`) should be enabled for schedule matrices to eliminate alignment jitter during live time updates.
- **Labels & Badges (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** High-weight, compact tracking designed for small-scale status pills (`RAC 12`, `TATKAL OPEN`, `3A`, `CC`).

### Contrast & Hierarchy Standards
- **Primary Text (`#F8FAFC`):** Station codes, train titles, active fare numbers, and arrival times.
- **Secondary Text (`#94A3B8`):** Scheduled running days, coach types, route distances, and intermediate junction names.
- **Tertiary & Muted Text (`#64748B`):** Disclaimer text, platform notice markers, and disabled indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a predictable, 8px-based baseline rhythm that maintains rigorous information density while preventing cognitive overload during critical reservation sequences.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with max-width constrained to `1320px` centered, utilizing `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Layouts partition into an asymmetric split: 4 columns for search controls, quota filters, and date pickers; 8 columns for train inventory lists, schedule comparisons, and coach map drawers.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Search controls collapse into horizontal sticky filter strips.
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas margins and `1rem` gutters. Interfaces stack into single-column operational streams with pinned bottom action docks.

### Spacing Cadence
- **`space-xs` (4px):** Micro gaps between icons and labels, berth pill internal badges, and stepper node rings.
- **`space-sm` (8px):** Internal element spacing within data groups (e.g., station code to station time).
- **`space-md` (16px):** Standard component padding for inputs, list items, and class-picker chips.
- **`space-lg` (24px):** Internal card padding for train cards and journey summaries.
- **`space-xl` (40px):** Vertical spacing separating independent search sections, timeline phases, and modal modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy in this dark system is established through a combination of surface tonal layers, calibrated cyan edge illumination, and deep-tinted glassmorphism rather than muddy, light-mode drop shadows.

### Depth Stratification
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas - `#0B1020`):** The structural grounding layer. Flat, absorbing, non-interactive.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Timetable Panels - `#141B2D`):** Resting cards bordered by `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`. Soft dark depth: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.5)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered & Active Items - `#1E293B`):** Raised interactive containers. Rimmed by subtle cyan luminescence: `border: 1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`, backed by `box-shadow: 0px 8px 30px -4px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.7), 0px 0px 12px 0px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Docks & Station Quick-Bars):** Deep navy glass `rgba(20, 27, 45, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, framed with `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`. Shadow: `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals, Coach Selectors & PNR Inspect Overlays):** `#141B2D` surface layered over an ultra-dark backdrop (`rgba(11, 16, 32, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`). Bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Shadow: `0px 24px 60px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9)`.

## Shapes

The design system operates at roundedness level `2` (Balanced Rounded). This geometry delivers an authoritative, technical framework balanced with accessible modern ergonomics.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **`rounded-sm` (0.25rem / 4px):** Internal tags, berth occupancy indicators, class abbreviation tags (`3A`, `2S`), and tabular timeline tick markers.
- **`rounded-md` (DEFAULT - 0.5rem / 8px):** Form fields, date selection tiles, seat grid boxes, quota segment controls, and secondary buttons.
- **`rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px):** Primary train cards, fare summary sheets, interactive route diagrams, and filter sidebars.
- **`rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px):** Floating search engines, PNR lookup dialogs, and persistent booking bar trays.
- **`rounded-full` (9999px):** Live running status indicator badges, station-swap swap buttons, step tracker nodes, and primary action pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Telemetry Button (Book Berth / Confirm PNR):** Electric Cyan background (`#38BDF8`), high-contrast dark text (`#0B1020`), `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold font (600), `rounded-md` or `rounded-full`, height 48px. Features a crisp cyan perimeter sheen. On hover: background `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, with `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`. Active state compresses slightly via `transform: scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Glass Button (Select Quota / Fare Breakup):** Background `#1E293B`, text `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`. On hover: border color transitions to `#38BDF8` with background tint `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost Button (Refresh Schedule / Rules):** Transparent background, text `#94A3B8`. Hover transitions to text `#38BDF8` with subtle background highlight `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.5)`.

### Status Badges & Pills
- **Available / Confirmed (CNF):** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, `rounded-full`, `label-sm` uppercase.
- **RAC & Tatkal Active:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#F59E0B`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`, `rounded-full`, `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Waitlist (WL / Regret):** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#EF4444`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`, `rounded-full`, `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Live Running Telemetry:** Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, text `#38BDF8`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`, featuring an animated pulsing dot for real-time tracking.

### Train Journey Cards
- **Card Container:** `#141B2D` surface, `rounded-lg`, border `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`. Hover transitions border to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`.
- **Header:** Left side displays train name in `headline-sm` with 5-digit train number in `label-md` `#94A3B8`. Right side shows running frequency indicator pills.
- **Station Timeline Row:** Origin code in `headline-md` (`#F8FAFC`) with time above; dynamic horizontal SVG rail line displaying travel duration (`06h 15m`) and intermediate stops; destination station code and arrival time.
- **Class Availability Grid:** Horizontal strip of tier buttons (`1A`, `2A`, `3A`, `SL`). Unselected items sit on `#1E293B` with status pills beneath. Selected class takes active border `#38BDF8` with glowing outline.

### Input Fields & Station Pickers
- **Containers:** Background `#0B1020`, border `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`, `rounded-md`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Station Swap Button:** Floating circle (`rounded-full`) positioned between origin and destination inputs. Background `#1E293B`, icon `#38BDF8`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`. Rotates 180 degrees with smooth spring easing on click.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts directly to `#38BDF8`, accompanied by an outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Segment Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Custom 20x20px square with `rounded-sm` corners. Resting border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.4)`. Checked state fills `#38BDF8` with deep navy `#0B1020` check glyph.
- **Segmented Quota Controls (General / Tatkal / Ladies / Senior):** Enclosed track on `#0B1020` with `rounded-md`. Active segment pops with `#1E293B` background, white text, and subtle cyan perimeter highlight.

### Live Route Telemetry Tracker
- **Vertical Stepper Path:** 2px vertical guide rail using `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`. Passed stations display solid `#10B981` ring nodes. Current station features a glowing `#38BDF8` ping animation. Upcoming stations show muted `#64748B` hollow circles. Inline data columns display scheduled time vs. actual delay deltas in high-contrast tabular `Inter`.
